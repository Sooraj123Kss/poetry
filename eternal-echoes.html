Eternal Echoes

    In the quiet of the twilight's glow,
    Whispers of the past begin to flow.
    Echoes of the laughter, tears we've known,
    In the heart's deep chambers softly strown.

    Through the corridors of time we tread,
    Guided by the voices of the dead.
    In the echoes, we find our way,
    Through the night and into day.

    Oh, the echoes never truly fade,
    In the heart and mind, their marks are made.
    Eternal echoes, soft and clear,
    In the silence, we always hear.
